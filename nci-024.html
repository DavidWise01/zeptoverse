Finite Reservoirs · Homeostatic Thermal Feedback

Cold and hot reservoirs now have finite capacities. Their temperatures respond to Peltier pumping, Joule heating and passive thermal leakage, while a balanced-ternary controller chooses PUMP (−1), COAST (0) or STOP (+1).

Conserved energy and entropy



The STOP state assumes an ideal insulated switch. Real devices leak heat, and a practical controller needs finite switching speed, measurement noise, and thermal contact losses. This is a deterministic finite-reservoir simulation, not a Casimir-powered thermal engine or measured Ice IX behavior.
