What this does not claim
Rigid rotation does not itself change eigenvalues. The model's orientation dependence is deliberately introduced through g(θ) = g₀ [1 + a cos(2θ)], which represents an anisotropic interaction. A physically predictive rotating cavity would require an explicit field equation, boundary coupling and rotating-frame treatment.
The periodic torroid is a mathematical flat T³ mode; the visible doughnut is a stylized cross-section. The signed cosmic background −1, reference 0, and positive departure +1 are categorical labels, not signed Kelvin or measured curvature.
