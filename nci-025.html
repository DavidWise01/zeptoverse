Thermal Memory · Hysteresis and Predictive Feedback

Two layers: physical reservoirs obey the same NCI-024 thermal laws; the control ledger independently learns the observed cooling slope and uses hysteresis to resist rapid switching. All choices, forecasts and prediction errors remain in the event history.

Prediction quality and thermodynamic audit

The model learns a local temperature slope for a synthetic, deterministic simulation—not a new physical law. Prediction quality need not improve in every generation when operating modes or temperatures change.
