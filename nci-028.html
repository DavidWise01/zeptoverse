What the signature means

Cryptographic verification: the receipt matches the private key corresponding to an operator-pinned public key. Signed fields include event, source, measurement, temperature, time, nonce, and upstream ancestry.

Not proven: these keys are generated in the same browser session. Their different signatures do not establish three real sensors, calibrated thermometers, trusted clocks, or physically independent observers.

The simulator intentionally leaves physical-witness trust false. No physical 1/1 END END seal is issued.

Scope and next measurement

Use an authenticated device-provisioning process with separate hardware keys, external clock discipline, recorded calibration, and upstream instrument provenance before considering a real checkpoint.

Neither the signatures nor the balanced-ternary controller prove Planck foam, Ice IX behavior, or a Casimir-powered device.
