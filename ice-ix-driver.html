Ice IX · The Hydrogen-Lattice Driver

Compare a disordered proton-routing toy model with an ordered 2-donor / 2-acceptor local rule. Ice IX is the real proton-ordered counterpart of ice III, but this graph is illustrative, not a crystallographic reconstruction.

Physical reference

Water ice forms extended hydrogen-bond networks. Ice IX is tetragonal and proton-ordered with some experimentally observed residual disorder. In the idealized ice-rule description, each oxygen has four neighboring bonds and two donated/two accepted hydrogens.
