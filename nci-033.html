Physics and its limits
For a massless scalar field, the Dirichlet cube admits positive mode indices with k = π√(n²)/L. The flat periodic three-torus admits signed integer mode indices with k = 2π√(n²)/L, including a zero-frequency mode that must be treated separately.
These eigenmodes are not a renormalized Casimir energy or stress tensor. The finite mode cutoff is computational; subtracting infinite vacuum mode sums would require additional geometry-aware renormalization and regularization.
At zeptometer or Planck lengths, literal material box boundaries are not physically validated. This experiment demonstrates a mathematical change in allowed spectra under different boundary conditions.
